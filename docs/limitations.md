# Limitations and Responsible Use

- Public benchmark datasets may not represent current clinical populations.
- Dataset labels and measurements can contain errors or selection bias.
- A random hold-out split does not prove generalization to another hospital, geography or time period.
- Accuracy alone is insufficient, especially for imbalanced health outcomes; report sensitivity, specificity, precision, calibration and subgroup performance where data permits.
- Risk bands shown in a demo are not clinical thresholds.
- No model output should be interpreted as a diagnosis, treatment recommendation or substitute for a clinician.
- Do not upload identifiable health records. Obtain appropriate permissions, governance review and security controls before handling real patient data.
- Clinical use would require prospective/external validation, calibration, fairness assessment, monitoring and applicable regulatory review.
